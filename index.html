A little of my story

I started at a tiny startup in Mumbai, working a pop-up stall and selling juice. Somewhere between the stall and the office, I realised marketing is a creative act, and that I liked it.

From there I moved into analytics at IIFL, then to Toronto. These days I’m at Publicis Media, reading what competitors are up to and measuring whether campaigns worked. Six years in, the part I still love is finding the pattern nobody else saw.

Where I’ve worked

This is where I am today. Keep going, and you can see where I began.

  • I built a competitive intelligence system. It reverse-engineers what competitors do with their ads, spend and media mix, and it grew into a new revenue stream for the agency. I run category and audience analysis, and lead competitive intelligence for new-business pitches. I present the findings to clients myself.
  • I built automated media-data workflows with layered checks, so planning teams got cleaner, standardised data. I also read TV performance data to show how brands and competitors were doing on air.
  • I led the response to Google’s cookie changes. I assessed consent tools and improved how audiences were captured. I also fixed attribution issues that skewed traffic reports, and built dashboards that aligned campaign KPIs across fundraising and media.
  • I connected CRM, web and sales data into one automated customer journey, so funnel performance was clear. I also built an attribution framework that turned marketing data into investment decisions. I coordinated a 15-person team and outside agencies.
  • I built the brand’s first data setup, so sales and marketing were visible in one place during the launch. I shaped the go-to-market strategy, designed its social identity, and led media buying.
