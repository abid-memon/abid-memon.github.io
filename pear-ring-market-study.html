The brief

Pear Ring was an upcoming brand with a launch ahead of it. Before that launch, the brand needed a clear read on where it stood and how it should show up.

The brief had four parts. Build the brand's digital and traditional presence. Understand consumer sentiment. Shape the launch strategy. And define the consumer persona and the brand goal, so the launch had a clear audience and a clear purpose to aim at.

My job was to do the research behind those answers: look at the brand itself, look at the brands around it, and read how it was already presenting itself in public.

What I did

  • 1.Researched the company websiteI started with the brand's own site, reading how Pear Ring described itself, what it offered, and how it was positioned before any launch activity.
  • 2.Assessed organic and paid impact in SEMrush and AhrefsUsing SEMrush and Ahrefs, I assessed the brand's organic and paid search impact and identified the competitors it appeared to be up against.
  • 3.Studied campaigns in Facebook Ad LibraryIn Facebook Ad Library, I studied the campaigns connected to the brand and the goals those campaigns seemed to be working toward.
  • 4.Searched Google for other mentionsI used Google search to find other mentions of the brand, so the picture covered more than the brand's own channels.
